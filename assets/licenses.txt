## About

Made by Spacely Digital. Questions, credit corrections or removal requests: spacelydigital@gmail.com.

For fun and general information only. Positions can be late, incomplete or wrong, and many aircraft are not shown. Not for navigation, air traffic, safety or emergency use.

Airline, operator and aircraft-manufacturer names are trademarks of their owners, used only to identify aircraft. This app is not affiliated with or endorsed by any airline, manufacturer or data provider. Not affiliated with the FAA.

## Data

Aircraft data: adsb.fi, https://adsb.fi (scan the code on this page).

Flight routes: adsb.lol, from Virtual Radar Server standing data (CC0). Routes are community-sourced and may be wrong.

adsb.lol data is made available under the Open Database License (ODbL): opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL): openstreetmap.org/copyright

Vector tile schema © OpenMapTiles (openmaptiles.org), licensed under CC BY 4.0: creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0

Tiles served by OpenFreeMap: openfreemap.org

Aircraft photos: Planespotters.net. Each photo is credited "Photo © photographer / Planespotters.net" beside it.

Satellite imagery in the U.S.: Map services and data available from U.S. Geological Survey, National Geospatial Program.

Satellite imagery elsewhere: EOxCloudless https://cloudless.eox.at by EOX IT Services GmbH (Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2016 & 2017), licensed under CC BY 4.0: creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0. Served from maps.eox.at. The imagery is resized and darkened for this app.

Place search: GeoNames via Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0). Time zones for addresses: Open-Meteo.

Approximate location: GeoJS (geojs.io), using GeoLite2 data created by MaxMind (maxmind.com)

Address matching: U.S. Census Bureau Geocoder (not endorsed by the Census Bureau)

Other addresses: Nominatim, © OpenStreetMap contributors.
